Prison Outreach

Jesus said, “I was in prison, and ye came unto me” (Matthew 25:36). In Douala, Cameroon, we have taken the love of Christ behind prison walls — praying with inmates, sharing the Gospel and reminding men and women that they are not forgotten by God.

  • Prayer and ministry of the Word with inmates
  • Encouragement and hope for those who feel forgotten
  • Practical care to meet everyday needs
Supported mission

Rural Missions

Some of the most faithful Gospel work happens far from the city lights. We have supported rural missions so that remote communities can hear the Good News and experience the practical love of God.

  • Support for missionaries serving remote communities
  • Prayer covering for the mission field
  • Helping the Gospel reach places that are often overlooked
In progress

Our Orphanage

“A father of the fatherless… is God in his holy habitation. God setteth the solitary in families” (Psalm 68:5–6). We are working toward an orphanage — a safe, loving, Christ-centred home where children can be cared for, educated and raised to know they are loved.

  • A safe home and daily care for orphaned children
  • Education, mentoring and discipleship
  • A family built on the love of Christ
