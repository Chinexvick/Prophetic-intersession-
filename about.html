About us

A house of prayer without walls

We pray, we reach, and we watch God transform lives — one altar, one prison cell, one village and one child at a time.

Our story

Born on the altar of prayer

Prophetic Intercession began with a simple conviction: nothing of eternal value happens without prayer. What started as a burden in the heart of our founder has grown into a gathering of intercessors who meet online from different nations and time zones to seek God together.

As we prayed, God began to open our eyes to people around us — the prisoner who feels forgotten, the rural village far from help, the child without a family. Our prayers became footsteps. Today, intercession and outreach move together in everything we do.

Mission & Vision

Praying

To raise a people of fervent, prophetic prayer who stand in the gap for families, churches, cities and nations, and who cry out for revival.

Reaching

To take the love of Jesus beyond the prayer room — to prisons, rural communities and children who are too often overlooked.

Transforming Lives

To see lives, families and communities transformed by the power of God, through prayer that is matched with practical compassion.

What we believe

The Word of God

We believe the Bible is the inspired Word of God and our foundation for faith, prayer and life.

Jesus Christ

We believe salvation is found in Jesus Christ alone, through His death and resurrection.

The Holy Spirit

We believe in the person and power of the Holy Spirit, who leads us in prayer and still speaks today.

Prophetic prayer

We believe God hears and answers prayer, and that intercession shaped by His voice changes outcomes.

Unity in prayer

We believe that when believers agree in prayer across denominations and nations, heaven responds.

Faith in action

We believe true prayer produces love in action — care for the prisoner, the poor and the fatherless.

How we gather

Online prayer altar

Our prayers are online — on Zoom, TikTok and YouTube — so intercessors anywhere in the world can join, from Africa to North America and beyond.

Prayer stretches on Zoom

Extended seasons of prayer like our 24 Hours Prayer Stretch, held on Zoom, where intercessors take turns keeping the fire burning.

Outreach missions

Prayer-led outreaches into prisons and rural communities, and the ongoing work of building an orphanage.
