Our sevenfold mandate

Raising a prophetic & intercessory tribe

Seven assignments that shape everything we pray for and every place we go.

01

Raising a Prophetic and Intercessory Generation

To raise, disciple and equip a consecrated people who understand the ministry of prayer, develop intimacy with God, discern His voice and faithfully stand in the gap for individuals, families, communities and nations.

Ezekiel 22:30 · 1 Timothy 2:1–4 · Isaiah 62:6–7
02

Birthing and Sustaining Genuine Revival

To become a spiritual catalyst for revival through persistent intercession, consecration, repentance and the stirring of spiritual hunger, raising people who will contend for a fresh move of God in the Church and across the nations.

Habakkuk 3:2 · Acts 2:1–4 · Joel 2:28–29 · 2 Chronicles 7:14
03

Awakening Gifts, Callings and Spiritual Destinies

To create an atmosphere of spiritual growth, impartation, discipleship and prophetic activation where believers discover, develop and deploy their God-given gifts, callings and assignments for kingdom advancement.

2 Timothy 1:6 · 1 Timothy 4:14 · Ephesians 4:11–13 · Romans 12:6–8
04

Demonstrating the Supernatural Reality of the Risen Christ

To cultivate a community that believes, pursues and demonstrates the supernatural ministry of the Holy Spirit through the gifts of the Spirit, divine healing, miracles, signs and wonders, bringing glory to Jesus Christ.

Mark 16:17–20 · Acts 4:29–33 · Hebrews 2:3–4 · 1 Corinthians 2:4–5
05

Evangelism

To labour in intercession for the salvation of the lost and translate this burden into intentional evangelism that brings people into the knowledge of Jesus Christ.

Romans 10:1 · 2 Corinthians 5:18–20 · 2 Corinthians 4:3 · Matthew 28:19–20
06

A Global Network of Prayer and Spiritual Collaboration

To unite believers, prayer movements, churches and ministries across denominations, cultures and nations into a global fellowship of intercessors, fostering corporate prayer, prophetic accountability and kingdom partnerships.

John 17:20–23 · Acts 1:14 · Matthew 18:19–20 · Ephesians 4:3–6
07

The Compassion of Christ Through Social Impact

To translate spiritual convictions into tangible expressions of Christ’s love by reaching the vulnerable, helping the helpless, supporting disadvantaged communities and initiating humanitarian projects that restore dignity.

Isaiah 58:6–12 · James 1:27 · Matthew 25:35–40 · Acts 10:38
