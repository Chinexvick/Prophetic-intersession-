Our convener

PropheticIntercessor

Our convener is a woman of prayer with a prophetic burden for revival. In the wake of 2020, God birthed in her the vision of Prophetic Intercession — and from 2023 she began gathering people on YouTube for extended hours of prayer, standing in the gap for individuals, families and nations.

Under her leadership the ministry has reached into prison in Douala, supported rural missions and begun the work of building an orphanage.

Her heart

“We are not raising people who merely know how to pray. We are raising people who know the God to whom they pray.”

Her foundational conviction is simple: everything by priesthood.
